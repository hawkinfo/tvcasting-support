TVCasting Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes TVCasting for iPhone and iPad, including its share and screen broadcast extensions. The publisher is individual developer zhifei Tao. For privacy questions, contact zifeit@163.com. Third-party websites, media providers and receivers have their own policies.

2. Your media and playback history

Videos, photos and subtitles that you import are copied to the app's storage on your device. Playback history may include titles, source URLs, import dates and saved positions. Receiver history can include the receiver's name, model, protocol and network address. These records help you find content and reconnect. Do not import or share content you do not want the selected receiver to access.

3. Local network and casting

The app discovers and communicates with receivers on your network. For local media, it creates temporary, randomly addressed HTTP resources for the selected media and subtitles. It does not expose your whole media library through that server. Local HTTP traffic is not end-to-end encrypted: use a network and receivers you trust. A temporary address is revoked when the local server stops; revocation cannot remove content already received by a TV or another device.

4. Screen broadcasting and audio

Broadcasting begins only after you use Apple's system broadcast interface. Screen content may include notifications and personal information visible in other apps. When you enable app audio, that audio is also encoded for the receiver. The app hides the microphone option and does not intentionally include microphone audio. Encoded screen segments are held in a bounded memory window, rather than saved as a recording by the app. Stop broadcasting through the system controls or the app. Protected content may be blank or unavailable.

5. Websites, remote media and third parties

The built-in browser uses a non-persistent WebKit data store. Website data can exist during browsing; this is not anonymous browsing. Websites and remote media providers receive ordinary requests, including network information such as your IP address, and may apply their own cookies and privacy practices. Your receiver may fetch a media URL directly. URLs saved in your history may contain sensitive query parameters. The app's media search does not export browser login cookies or credentials and does not bypass DRM.

6. Diagnostics

The app keeps up to 150 diagnostic events in memory. Events contain a random event identifier, time, stage and outcome. They exclude media, filenames, full URLs, local network addresses and receiver names. There is no automatic upload of these events. Exporting diagnostics creates a temporary JSON file; the app shares it only when you choose a destination. You can clear diagnostics in Settings, which also removes the app's temporary diagnostic export. Copies you have already shared must be managed with the recipient.

7. SDKs, analytics and advertising

The app includes Google Cast, GCDWebServer, CocoaAsyncSocket and bundled Protobuf components. Optional Google Cast analytics logging is disabled. The app does not include an advertising integration or app-operated cross-app tracking. Google SDKs, Apple services, websites and receiver platforms may process data under their own policies. Disabling optional SDK analytics does not disable all SDK or platform traffic. Google's policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Our public privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process visitor network information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement: GitHub Privacy Statement. These static pages contain no publisher-added analytics, advertising scripts or contact forms.

8. App settings and extensions

Language, playback and storage preferences are saved locally. Language preference is shared with the app's extensions through the app's private App Group. Content saved with the share extension waits in the shared inbox until the main app successfully imports it; its inbox copy is then removed. If an import fails, the pending copy may remain for another attempt.

9. Retention, deletion and backups

Imported copies and playback history remain until you delete the relevant items in Recents or clear imported media in Settings. Receiver history has a separate clear control. To remove pending shared content, return to the app, let it import, then delete the imported item. Clear diagnostics separately. Deleting an imported copy does not delete the original in Photos or Files. Prepared temporary media is removed when replaced or playback is stopped; the operating system may also clear temporary storage. Device backups may include local app data according to your Apple backup settings. TVCasting does not operate a cloud media sync service. Removing local data does not erase copies on receivers, content services, backups or destinations you chose to share with.

10. Permissions and choices

Local network permission supports discovery and local streaming. System Photos and Files pickers provide the items you select, rather than requiring access to your entire library. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. Declining local network permission can limit Cast and DLNA features; system AirPlay availability is managed by iOS. Local Cast and DLNA file serving currently requires this app to remain in the foreground.

11. Accounts, payments and requests

This version does not require a TVCasting account and has no in-app purchase flow. The publisher does not receive your local library or these diagnostic events automatically. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal data. Use the local deletion controls or contact the publisher using the privacy contact provided below or on the App Store page. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, including any attachments. We use this information to respond and resolve your request and retain it only as needed for support, applicable legal obligations and dispute handling. You may email zifeit@163.com to request deletion, subject to applicable requirements. The email provider also processes messages under its own policies. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or private media with a support request.

12. Children and updates

The app is a general-purpose media tool and is not designed specifically for children. Parents and guardians should supervise content selection and broadcasting. This policy may change when app behavior changes; the updated date identifies the current text. Material changes involving new data uses require updated disclosure and consent where applicable.
