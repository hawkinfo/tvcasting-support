TVCasting Help & Support

Getting started

Choose a video, photo or file, then choose an output. For Apple TV and AirPlay TVs, select the system AirPlay output. For Google Cast, use the official Cast picker. For DLNA, enable the TV's media receiver and search in the app. Keep the phone and receiver on the same trusted network.

If no TV appears

Wake the TV and enable its receiver feature. Check local network permission in iOS Settings. Guest Wi-Fi, client isolation and VPNs can prevent discovery. Different protocols have different receiver requirements; a TV brand alone does not establish compatibility.

Playback and formats

Controls depend on the receiver. For a local video that fails to play, try the one-time compatibility conversion. Conversion takes time and extra storage and does not modify the original. Protected media, login-only media and browser blob URLs may require the content provider's official app.

Background and screen mirroring

Local Cast and DLNA media serving requires TVCasting to stay in the foreground. Returning to the app lets you explicitly resume from the saved position where the receiver supports seeking. System AirPlay is managed by iOS. Cast and DLNA screen mirroring uses Apple's broadcast interface and a receiver that supports the live stream; buffering adds delay. Test audio, orientation and temperature on your own devices.

Preparing a support request

Include the app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, receiver model and firmware, connection method and steps to reproduce. You may choose to export diagnostic events from Settings. Never include passwords, authentication tokens, private media, signed media links or screen content containing personal information. Diagnostics are not sent automatically.

Contact

Use the support contact or website shown below, or the support details on TVCasting's App Store page. Responses and compatibility depend on the publisher's support arrangements. Existing diagnostic exports and imported media can be cleared separately in Settings.
